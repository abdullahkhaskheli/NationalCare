# Visual assets

The visual layout uses the starter's green healthcare theme and discovery/institution sections inspired by https://studyinsaudi.sa/en. It is an independent implementation, not an official government website.

Hospital names, locations and logos identify public directory entries. Their owners retain their rights; listing does not imply partnership or endorsement. Sources:

- Aga Khan University Hospital: https://hospitals.aku.edu/pakistan/facility/akuh-main-campus
- Shaukat Khanum: https://shaukatkhanum.org.pk/contact-us/
- Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences: https://pims.gov.pk/ContactUs.php
- Sindh Institute of Urology and Transplantation: https://siut.org/

The three healthcare photographs were downloaded from Unsplash for the public discovery sections. They are illustrative stock images and do not identify patients in this system. Review asset licensing/brand permissions for your final distribution.

Arabic/Urdu fonts are locally hosted. Amiri is used for PDF Arabic shaping; see https://github.com/aliftype/amiri for its SIL Open Font License and provenance. The supplied Arabic web font should retain its upstream license when redistributed.
